## Paid Search Built on Commercial Intent, Not Wasted Clicks

Google Ads can either be an extraordinary growth driver or an expensive black hole for your marketing budget. Most campaigns fail because agencies set broad-match keywords, enable automated display network expansions, and burn money on irrelevant searches.

At SeoBay India, Google Ads is managed with the same **search intent rigor** as our organic search practice.

### Our Disciplined PPC Methodology

### 1. High-Intent Commercial Structuring
We target searchers at the bottom of the funnel—prospects actively looking for suppliers, showrooms, specialists, or service providers. Every keyword is screened for commercial buying intent before a single rupee of ad spend is committed.

### 2. Aggressive Negative Keyword Governance
The secret to high-ROI paid search is what you *prevent* from triggering your ads. We build exhaustive negative keyword lists before launch and conduct weekly search term query audits to eliminate wasted budget.

### 3. Pristine Conversion Measurement
We implement verified conversion tracking (form submissions, telephone calls, WhatsApp chats) and verify that Google's bidding algorithm optimizes for qualified inquiries rather than accidental clicks.

### 4. Organic & Paid Search Synergy
By managing both paid search and organic SEO, we identify which commercial keywords convert best and use that intelligence to inform your long-term organic content strategy.
