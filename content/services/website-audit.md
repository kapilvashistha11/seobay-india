## Stop Guessing Why Your Website Isn't Ranking

Most agencies sell "audits" that are nothing more than automated 50-page PDF exports from tools like Semrush or Ahrefs. They give you hundreds of trivial warnings, zero contextual analysis, and no clear explanation of what actually moves the needle.

At SeoBay India, every technical audit is **manually conducted by Kapil Vashistha** (16 years practitioner depth). We look at your codebase, crawl logs, indexation status, and Core Web Vitals from the perspective of how search engines actually crawl and understand your site.

### What Our Diagnostic Uncovers

### 1. Crawlability & Indexation Traps
* Are search engine bots wasting crawl budget on duplicate parameters, search filters, or faceted navigation?
* Do you have orphaned URLs that search engines cannot discover without XML sitemap crutches?
* Are your canonical tags resolving properly across HTTP/HTTPS, trailing slashes, and subdomains?

### 2. Core Web Vitals & Rendering Bottlenecks
* Measuring real user field metrics: Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Interaction to Next Paint (INP), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
* Identifying heavy JavaScript bundles, unoptimized web fonts, and render-blocking scripts that degrade mobile user experience and organic rank eligibility.

### 3. Entity Clarity & Structured Data
* Inspecting whether Google recognizes your company as a verified entity or an isolated collection of keywords.
* Auditing `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, and breadcrumb markup for compliance with Google Search Essentials.

### 4. Commercial Intent Alignment
* Auditing whether your current pages match commercial search intent or if you are cannibalizing rankings across duplicate landing pages.
